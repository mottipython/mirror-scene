# Mirror Scene: Gemini prompts

Copy each block whole into Gemini and save the result in `~/Pictures/mirror-scene/` under the name in the heading.

## 1. The room: two pieces

The mirror itself is built by `tools/compose_room.py`; Gemini only supplies the pieces.

### 1a. bedroom.png (an edit: upload the Art Deco bedroom picture with this)

```

Change nothing else: keep the windows, curtains, lamps, tables, rug, colours, lighting and style exactly as they are, and keep the same size and framing.
```
